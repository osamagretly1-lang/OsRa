OsRa R67 legacy-dance restoration assets

These 26 transparent WebP frames are derived from existing R66 assets, not newly invented character art.
The timeline includes all 14 distinct historical paired poses and 11 supplemental R62 poses. The twelfth supplemental file, pose-11.webp, remains untouched in the source folder but is not used in the active timeline because its original image visibly cuts off the top of both heads. Its raised-arm beat is represented by a head-safe original raised-arms pose instead. That safe pose appears twice in the sequence; this is preferable to showing clipped heads.
Only transparent outer margins of each selected source were trimmed. Visible source pixels were preserved and uniformly scaled onto a common 420x660 transparent canvas, improving mobile display without cutting heads, hands, clothing, shoes, or the wide carrying/leaning poses.
The frame order alternates original couple poses with supplemental turn, dip, lift/carry, embrace/lean and raised-arm poses. The app loops the full sequence until the configured dance duration ends.
All historical source assets, including the excluded defective pose-11.webp, remain in their original directories and are not overwritten.
